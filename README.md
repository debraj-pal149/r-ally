# r-ally

**The app that shouts at you at the exact moment you want to quit — so you don't.**

v1 is a **continuous-effort coach**. It watches live output (pace / power) against heart rate and cadence, detects the fade *before* the walk break, and puts a short pep line in your earbuds.

> **Phone-only running is the product.** GPS pace, pedometer cadence, and grade — no watch or strap required. On a physical iPhone the app defaults to **iPhone sensors** (not Simulator). Apple Health is used to **save** the finished run after you authorize it — it is not the live tracking pipe. Live tracking is the phone’s GPS + cadence. When you slow or stop, you get a shout; if you pick up, a recovery line; if you stay stopped, a firmer follow-up. Ride/row stay in the engine/simulator only. Strength/HIIT/boxing/swim are in [`future/v2`](future/v2/README.md).

The voice is a corner man (Rocky / Les Brown / Arnold / drill sergeant / zen), generated by Claude when the key is present, otherwise from a built-in bank, spoken on-device.

## 1. Product vision

### The problem

Every hard run has a moment — usually more than one — where output sags *before* the athlete decides to stop. Pace slips. Cadence gets ragged. Heart rate stays pinned or climbs. That window is typically **15–60 seconds**. A well-timed voice inside it is the highest-leverage intervention in amateur endurance. Coaches do it. Corner men do it. Crowds at mile 25 do it.

### The insight that makes this buildable

You cannot measure "wants to quit." You can measure **effort decay relative to the athlete's own recent baseline**. The signature of struggling (output falling *while* HR stays high) is distinguishable from a deliberate stop (output falls *and* HR is allowed to fall). That is aerobic decoupling — the same idea as TrainingPeaks Pw:HR.

That signature exists for **continuous locomotor sports**. It does **not** exist for lifting, HIIT, or boxing: rest is the protocol. Pointing a fade detector at a lifter fires constantly or never. Those sports leak a different signal (rest creep / late-to-the-next-interval) and live in v2.

A missed shout costs nothing. A shout on a hill, a traffic light, or a programmed rest kills the product. **Precision over recall, everywhere.**

### Product feeling

- **Audio-first.** Phone in a pocket. The product is the voice.
- **Corner man, not a notification.** Music ducks, the line lands, music returns.
- **The moment must be right.** One timed line beats ten scheduled ones.
- **Rally Moments** after the session: "Kilometer 3.4 — you were fading. You rallied."
- **The name:** rally + ally. Big button: **"Let's Rally."**

### Non-goals for v1

- No accounts, no backend, no social, no Android.
- No on-device ML. Classical signal processing, tuned on simulator ground truth.
- No gym/HIIT/boxing/swim detection in the shipped app.
- No claiming GPS-only cycling is accurate.

## 2. What we are building

### Artifact A — the r-ally app

SwiftUI, iOS 17+, Xcode 26.5 / iOS 26 SDK.

- Real outdoor runs: GPS pace, pedometer cadence, barometric **grade-adjusted pace**, optional BLE / HealthKit HR. No watch required for running.
- Ride and row on device: **blocked** until a heart-rate strap is paired. Simulator sessions always allowed (the stream already has HR/power).
- Mac demo: iOS Simulator + local WebSocket to Artifact B.
- All inputs are `MetricSample`. The engine does not know the source.
- Quit-Risk Engine at 1 Hz. Anthropic `claude-haiku-4-5` for pep lines. `AVSpeechSynthesizer` through a short room (EQ + compressor + reverb), preferring Siri / Premium voices.
- Reports risk and triggers back to the simulator for the dashboard overlay.
- Saves workouts to HealthKit; optional Strava upload after the fact.

### Artifact B — the activity simulator

TypeScript Node + React dashboard in `simulator/`.

- Physiologically-plausible streams for seven activities (engine test coverage).
- Scripted JSON scenarios with **hidden** ground-truth quit events.
- 1 Hz WebSocket, time scale 1×–30×.
- Dashboard groups scenarios: **Shipped** (run / ride / row) vs **Lab only** (do not attach the app).
- Rescuable-athlete mode: a well-timed shout recovers effort.
- CSV export for Swift tests; eval scorer for lead time / recall / false positives.

## 4. System architecture

```
simulator/ (Node :7777)  ──metrics──▶  r-ally (iOS)
                 ◀── risk, trigger, speechDone ──

DataSourceManager
  SimulatorDataSource | DeviceSensorDataSource
  + BLEHeartRateDataSource + HealthKitDataSource
        ▼
   MetricStore (1 Hz, EWMA, slope, CV)
        ▼
   QuitRiskEngine  (Running / Cycling / Rowing adapters)
                   SilentLabAdapter for parked sports
        ▼
   LineCache → Anthropic or FallbackLines → SpeechEngine (VoiceBooth)
```

Rules:

1. Everything is a `MetricSample(kind, value, timestamp, source)`.
2. No `if simulator` downstream of `MetricStore`.
3. Only the app talks to Anthropic.
4. Engine clock is message `t`, never wall clock (critical at 30×).
5. Lab scenarios must not shout. `SilentLabAdapter` sets `plannedRest = true`.

## 5. The Quit-Risk Engine

### 5.1 Triggers

| Kind | Signature | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| `pre_quit_fade` | Output dropping vs own baseline **while HR is pinned or rising**, or cadence falling apart with the drop | Interrupt the doubt |
| `stopped` | Output ~zero for longer than grace, HR still elevated, goal not done | Get them moving |
| `grind_support` | Holding hard with little drop | Fuel, not correction |
| `final_push` | ≥ 90% of declared distance | Bring it home |

Stay silent:

- Cooldown (output and HR both falling)
- Hills (grade-adjusted pace, or cadence still intact)
- GPS-only cycling/rowing (proxy output, no HR)
- Micro-pauses inside the 8 s grace window
- Lab sports (silent adapter)
- Warmup (first 180 s), refractory 90 s, session cap 7

### 5.2 Adapters (what feeds the core)

Every shipped adapter reduces to `(output, hr, rhythm)` plus `outputIsProxy`.

| Activity | `output` | `rhythm` | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Running | speed, **× (1 + 0.033 · grade%)** | cadence | Always startable. Phone-only fade requires cadence collapse, not pace drop alone. |
| Cycling | **power watts** if > 15 W; else GPS speed marked **proxy** | pedal cadence | Device: BLE strap required. Proxy + no HR → no fade. |
| Rowing | **power watts** if > 15 W; else speed proxy | stroke rate | Same gate as cycling. |
| strength / boxing / HIIT / swim | `SilentLabAdapter` | — | Never shouts. Real designs in `future/v2`. |

Cycling must **not** invent watts from speed (`speed × 30` was a lie). A climb looks identical to a bonk on GPS.

### 5.3 Features (1 Hz)

`MetricStore` per channel:

- `base(t)` — EWMA, 90 s half-life
- `short(t)` — 15 s SMA
- `slope30(t)` — 30 s least-squares slope
- `cv20(t)` — rhythm coefficient of variation, 20 s

```
outputDrop  = clamp((base_out − short_out) / max(base_out, ε), 0, 1)
decaySlope  = clamp(−slope30_out / (0.005 · base_out), 0, 1)
decoupling  = 0 if outputDrop ≤ 0.05 or no HR
            = max( rising HR, pinned-high HR · drop )
              rising = clamp(slope30_hr / 0.05, 0, 1)
              pinned = 0.75 if slope30_hr > −0.02 bpm/s else 0
              (flat-high HR while slowing is the common amateur fade)
cooldownCue = clamp(−slope30_hr / 0.15, 0, 1)
stutter     = clamp((cv20 − base_cv) / 0.10, 0, 1)
grind       = clamp(secondsHard / 300, 0, 1)
```

```
raw  = 100 · σ( 3.2·drop + 2.0·slope + 2.4·decoupling + 1.2·stutter
                + 0.6·grind − 2.8·cooldown − 2.2 )
risk = 0.7·prev + 0.3·raw
```

State machine:

```
CRUISING  ── risk ≥ 48 for 3 ticks ──▶ WOBBLING
WOBBLING  ── risk ≥ 68 for 4 ticks ──▶ CRITICAL  → maybe pre_quit_fade
WOBBLING  ── risk ≤ 35 for 10 ticks ──▶ CRUISING
CRITICAL  ── risk ≤ 50 for 15 ticks ──▶ WOBBLING
```

**Honesty gate on `pre_quit_fade` (a miss is free):**

- Planned rest or cooldown cue > 0.5 → no
- Proxy output and no HR → no
- HR present → need (decoupling ≥ 0.28 and drop ≥ 0.06) **or** (stutter ≥ 0.40 and drop ≥ 0.10)
- Phone-only running → stutter ≥ 0.45 and drop ≥ 0.12

**Sudden stop:** `short_out < 0.15·base` lasting past 8 s grace, HR still > 75% of session peak, goal not done → `stopped`.

Constants live in `EngineConstants.swift`. Tune there, not scattered.

### 5.4 Why not seven detectors

One detector over `(output, hr, rhythm)` is correct **when effort is continuous and quantified**. Interval sports need a clock and a declared plan. See `future/v2/README.md`.

## 6. Data sources

| Source | Live? | What we build |
|---|---|---|
| **Simulator** | yes, all metrics | `SimulatorDataSource` → `ws://127.0.0.1:7777/stream`. DEBUG default. Lab hellos do **not** switch the app onto boxing/lift/HIIT/swim. |
| **iPhone sensors** | pace, cadence, distance, **altitude/grade**, motion RMS. No HR. | `DeviceSensorDataSource`. Background `location` + `audio`. Speed clamped [0.3, 12] m/s; `horizontalAccuracy < 30`. |
| **BLE HR (GATT 0x180D)** | yes | `BLEHeartRateDataSource`. Required to start Ride/Row on device. |
| **HealthKit** | supplemental HR if something else is writing it; save workouts; NRC history if it synced | `HealthKitDataSource` + `HealthKitWriter`. `#if canImport(HealthKit)`. |
| **Apple Watch** | needs a companion we do not ship | `WatchConnectivitySource` stub. |
| **Strava** | no live API | Post-workout TCX upload. Hidden until `.env` has client id+secret. |
| **Garmin / Fitbit / NRC APIs** | no live consumer API | BLE broadcast for Garmin HR; NRC via HealthKit only. |

Fusion: user picks primary (Simulator / iPhone). BLE and HealthKit HR are supplemental. On conflict: BLE > Watch > HealthKit > Simulator > derived.

## 7. The Encouragement Engine

### Pipeline

```
risk ≥ 35 (WOBBLING)  → prefetch 3 lines × trigger kind from Claude
trigger               → pop cache → speak (< 700 ms)
                      → refill
offline / parse fail  → FallbackLines (≥ 40 × persona × kind)
```

Model: `claude-haiku-4-5`. `max_tokens: 300`, `temperature: 1.0`, 5 s timeout. Never retry on the trigger path.

### Personas

| id | Name | Archetype | Speech (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `southpaw` | The Southpaw | Rocky corner man | ~0.46 rate, ~0.98 pitch, male neural |
| `machine` | The Machine | Arnold-style | similar, slightly lower |
| `preacher` | The Preacher | Les Brown | slower, hall reverb |
| `sarge` | Sarge | Drill instructor | dry room, clipped |
| `steady` | The Steady | Zen | slower, female neural, hall air |

Voice ranking prefers Siri / Premium / Enhanced English, demotes compact Samantha and novelty voices. Playback goes through `VoiceBooth` (low-shelf body, cut the nasal 3 kHz, compressor, per-persona reverb). Rate/pitch jitter slightly per breath chunk.

Lines are written for the ear: vocative comma, em dash for a breath, ending period. Download a Siri or Premium English voice in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

### Prompt

Hard rules (verbatim in `PromptBuilder.swift`): ≤ 12 words; write for the ear; no quotes, emojis, sensors, AI, profanity, or "push through chest pain." Output JSON with `pre_quit_fade` / `stopped` / `grind_support` arrays.

Running chips (others analogous, shorter):

- Don't let me negotiate with myself
- Remind me why I started
- I'm training for my first 10K
- Talk to me like it's the last mile
- Tell me my legs are strong
- I run to be a good example for my kids
- Custom… / No prompt

## 8. The Activity Simulator

Physiology: effort `e(t)` ∈ [0,1] drives HR (lag + drift + noise), W′-lite fatigue, then sport-specific output. Seeded PRNG (`mulberry32`). Same seed + scenario = identical stream.

**Shipped scenarios** (app + eval claims):

- `run-easy-clean`, `run-5k-bonk`, `run-intervals`, `run-gradual-fade-late`
- `ride-tempo-fade`, `ride-surges`
- `row-2k-grind`

**Lab scenarios** (`"lab": true` — engine tests, dashboard optgroup, app will not follow activity):

- `lift-5x5-grind`, `lift-fast-quit`, `box-6-rounds-fade-r5`, `hiit-tabata-quit`, `swim-1500-dropouts`

Event kinds: `gradual_fade`, `sudden_stop`, `pause_resume` (distractor), `grind`, `interval_rest`. Rescuable events recover if a trigger lands inside `rescueWindowSec`.

```
pnpm dev                                  # :7777 + dashboard :5173
pnpm export --scenario run-5k-bonk --seed 42 --out ../fixtures/run-5k-bonk.csv
pnpm eval:report
```

CSV: `t,kind,value` plus `# event:` comments. Swift tests replay **shipped** CSVs in `fixtures/`. Lab CSVs live in `future/v2/fixtures/`.

Dashboard: dark athletic theme (Barlow Condensed over system sans). Shipped vs Lab optgroups. Badge `lab — don't attach the app` when a lab scenario is selected.

## 9. WebSocket protocol

Source of truth: `simulator/src/protocol.ts`, mirrored in `Simulator/Protocol.swift`. `PROTOCOL_VERSION = 1`.

- App: `ws://127.0.0.1:7777/stream`
- Dashboard: `ws://127.0.0.1:7777/dashboard` (gets ground truth; the app never does)

Metric kinds: `heartRateBpm, paceSecPerKm, speedMps, cadenceSpm, powerWatts, strokeRateSpm, punchRatePpm, repVelocityMps, repCount, motionIntensityG, distanceM, activeEnergyKcal`. Device-only extras (not on the wire today): `altitudeM`, `gradePercent`.

```jsonc
{ "v":1, "type":"hello", "scenarioId":"run-5k-bonk", "activity":"running", "timeScale":10, "tickHz":1 }
{ "v":1, "type":"metrics", "t":412.0, "samples":[{"k":"heartRateBpm","x":163.2},{"k":"paceSecPerKm","x":341.0}] }
{ "v":1, "type":"risk", "t":688.0, "score":58.2, "state":"WOBBLING" }
{ "v":1, "type":"trigger", "t":703.0, "kind":"pre_quit_fade", "persona":"southpaw", "text":"Kid, stay in it.", "sourceLLM":true, "latencyMs":214 }
```

Ping 15 s. Reconnect 1→2→4→8 s. Workout clock pauses while disconnected.

## 10. App UX

Single `NavigationStack`. Dark. Compressed athletic type (not serif, not rounded "AI" fonts). 44 pt minimum targets. Haptics on selection.

### Home

- Greeting + Sim/Phone pill → Settings.
- **Today:** three cards — Run (flagship), Ride, Row. Ride/Row show `HR / POWER`.
- Phone-only run: yellow banner. Ride/Row without strap on device: ember banner + CTA disabled ("Pair a strap first").
- Distance stepper (km).
- Persona row + Hear it (selecting a coach also previews the voice).
- Prompt chips.
- Pinned bottom **"Let's Rally"**.
- Last session card → Summary. History link.

### Live

Black. Elapsed time in the pulse ring (HR or cadence). State label HOLDING / FADING / RALLY. Three metric tiles. Pause / hold-to-end / mute. Spoken line overlays in compressed display type. Screen stays awake.

### Summary / History / Settings

Summary: distance, time, HR, rally timeline, Health / Strava, pinned Done. History: session list. Settings: Simulator vs iPhone, BLE pair, ranked voice list + Hear this coach, athlete, Health, optional Strava, shout cap. Consistent chevron back.

### Onboarding

Name → age → persona (earbuds copy) → permissions primer → Home.

## 17. Evaluation

`simulator/src/eval/scorer.ts` matches triggers to ground truth:

- Fade/grind/stop detected if a matching trigger lands in **[onset − 10 s, onset + 45 s]** (fade/grind) or **[onset + grace, onset + 30 s]** (stop).
- Trigger outside windows = false positive. Distractors (pause, planned rest, cooldown) weighted ×2.
- **Score shipped scenarios as the product.** Lab scenarios test the Node physiology engine, not the shipped detector.

Targets on the **shipped** suite (seed 42):

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Recall on fade/stop/grind | ≥ 0.85 |
| Weighted FPs | ≤ 1 / 30 sim minutes |
| Median fade lead | ≤ 25 s after onset |
| Distractor triggers | **0** |
| Trigger → first syllable | < 700 ms cached / < 150 ms fallback |

Tune `EngineConstants.swift` only.

## 19. Gotchas

1. ATS: `NSAllowsLocalNetworking` or `ws://localhost` fails in RELEASE.
2. Never route Mac demos through `DeviceSensorDataSource`.
3. HealthKit `#if canImport(HealthKit)`.
4. xcconfig `//` in API keys.
5. BLE HR flags bit 0 = uint8 vs uint16.
6. Engine clock is simulated `t`, not wall clock.
7. WebSocket receive loop must re-arm.
8. GPS: clamp speed, require accuracy < 30 m.
9. Hills: use grade-adjusted output; never shout on pace drop alone.
10. Do not attach the iOS app to a LAB scenario and expect a boxing detector. There isn't one.

## 20. Roadmap (v2)

| Sport | v2 mechanic |
